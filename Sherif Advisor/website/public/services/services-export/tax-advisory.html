Tax Advisory

Tax certainty for investing and operating in Egypt

Investors price uncertainty into every decision. In Egypt, tax is often the largest source of it: rules change frequently, the Egyptian Tax Authority now reviews filings through connected digital systems, and open tax years can stay open for a long time.

We give local companies and foreign investors a clear view of their tax position from set-up to exit. We keep compliance accurate, structure transactions before they happen, and represent clients when positions are challenged, so tax becomes a managed cost rather than a surprise.
