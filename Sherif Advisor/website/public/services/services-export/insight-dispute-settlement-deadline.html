The tax dispute settlement window closes on 31 December 2026

Sherif Yousry Tax Advisory | September 2026 | 3 min read

Law No. 79 of 2016 on the settlement of tax disputes has been extended to the end of 2026. Taxpayers must submit a settlement request by that date; the case itself does not need to be closed.

In June 2026 the House of Representatives approved the extension of Law No. 79 of 2016 on the settlement of tax disputes until 31 December 2026. The specialised settlement committees will continue to review pending cases and accept new applications until that date.

The Minister of Finance clarified that disputes do not have to be fully settled before 31 December. Taxpayers need only submit a request expressing their willingness to settle within the period.

Why this matters

Disputes before the Appeal Committees and the courts can take years to resolve, and late-payment charges continue to accrue while they are open. For investors, open disputes also weigh on valuations and extend due diligence.

Who should consider settlement

Companies with disputes pending before the Appeal Committees or the courts.

Buyers and sellers in a transaction who want a clean tax position at completion.

Groups preparing for a restructuring, a listing or an exit.

What to do before the deadline

List every open dispute, its amount and its current stage.

Compare the likely settlement outcome with the cost and time of continuing the dispute.

Assemble the supporting documents for each case.

Submit the request well before 31 December to leave time to resolve any procedural issues.

Eligibility is subject to the conditions set out in the law. We can review your open cases and tell you which are worth settling.
