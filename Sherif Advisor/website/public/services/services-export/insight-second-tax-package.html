Egypt's second tax facilitation package: what changes for companies

Sherif Yousry Tax Advisory | September 2026 | 4 min read

Eight laws ratified in July 2026 put the second package into effect. The measures target faster refunds, fewer disputes and lower compliance costs, without introducing new taxes.

In July 2026 the President ratified eight laws that make up the second package of tax facilitation measures, which the Minister of Finance said would apply immediately. The package builds on the first package, which focused on small businesses, and extends the same approach to larger companies and the capital market.

Liquidity: faster refunds and cash recovery

The package accelerates VAT refunds, expands the cases that qualify and raises the refundable amounts. Companies on a new White List of compliant taxpayers are to receive immediate refunds and priority services through a Distinguished Taxpayer Card. The Ministry has also announced that companies will be able to recover cash credit balances shown on their income tax returns, rather than only offsetting them against future tax.

Certainty: an end to estimated assessments

According to the Minister of Finance, estimated assessments are abolished for future tax periods. Existing disputes over estimated assessments for earlier years remain subject to the previous rules.

Measures by area

VAT: no VAT on transit goods and related services; exemption for non-bank financial services; the suspension of VAT on machinery, production equipment and medical devices extended from two to four years; and VAT on medical devices reduced from 14% to 5%.

Group structures: double taxation of dividends distributed between Egyptian parent and subsidiary companies removed, so tax is collected once.

Capital market: Law No. 151 of 2026 gives listed companies a 15% deduction from income tax due for three years, subject to conditions, and Law No. 153 of 2026 revises the stamp duty treatment of listed securities.

What companies should do now

Review your compliance record against the White List criteria once they are published.

Revisit pending VAT refund claims and income tax credit balances.

Map dividend flows within your Egyptian group and quantify the saving.

If you are listed or considering a listing, model the effect of the Law No. 151 deduction.

Several measures depend on executive regulations and instructions from the Egyptian Tax Authority. We will update this note as they are issued.

This note summarises announced measures for general information and is not advice on any specific situation. Contact us before acting on it.
